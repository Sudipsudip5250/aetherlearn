# Code of Conduct

AetherLearn is committed to a welcoming, respectful, and collaborative community. Participants should communicate in good faith, assume good intent while addressing impact, give and receive constructive feedback, and respect different backgrounds, abilities, experience levels, and learning goals.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, doxxing, deliberate disruption, unwanted sexual attention, publication of private information, and attempts to use the project to facilitate harm or unauthorized access. Do not submit credentials, personal data, exploit kits, malware, or instructions targeting real systems or people.

Project maintainers may edit, reject, close, or remove contributions that violate this code, the repository’s safety policy, or applicable platform rules. They may ask participants to pause or move a sensitive discussion to a private channel. Reports should include the relevant link, context, and preferred follow-up method, while avoiding unnecessary personal information or secrets.

For security vulnerabilities, follow [`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md) rather than opening a public issue. For dual-use content, follow [`docs/SAFETY.md`](docs/SAFETY.md). Maintainers will handle reports as confidentially as practical and will not retaliate against good-faith reporters.
